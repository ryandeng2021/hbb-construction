# Home page: public design retained

Owner direction: restore the published home page, its original cover carousel,
Welcome content, map statistics, Why Choose Us and Process, with the existing
navigation, footer and Free Quote. Under Welcome, replace the full project grid
with the three approved alternating featured residences (20, 24, 27). Follow
these with a compact View Our Portfolio ribbon showing all 29 thumbnails in a
continuous rightward loop; then continue directly to the original map section.

Portfolio and Gallery retain their revised layouts. All text uses the public
site's Cormorant Garamond family and original fallback stack, including controls
and body text. The original font is hosted locally from Google Fonts' official
repository under its included OFL license to avoid external-font loading gaps.
The editorial Georgia/Arial overrides are superseded. Original icon fonts and
SVG icons are unaffected.

The later concept construction animation and interactive guide are removed from
the home page in favor of the published homepage's original content. The guide's
standalone route and reviewed answer data remain available for future use.

The thumbnail ribbon pauses on hover, keyboard focus or its pause button.
Reduced-motion visitors get a manually scrollable row instead. Duplicate visual
items are hidden from accessibility navigation. The first group contains every
project once, linking to its existing detail URL.
